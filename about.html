Second Look Start playing

Learn chess free, with a coach that stops your blunders

Second Look is a chess website for complete beginners. It teaches you how the pieces move, gives you thousands of puzzles, and lets you play bots that start very easy. While you play, a coach watches every move and explains it in plain words.

Open Second Look

What you can do

  • Learn from zero

    21 short lessons: how each piece moves, check and checkmate, castling, forks, pins and how to start a game. You learn by moving the pieces yourself.

  • Play friendly bots

    Six levels, from a Rookie bot that makes lots of mistakes up to full-strength Stockfish. Play with or without a clock.

  • Coach Guard

    Before a big mistake, the coach stops you, shows the danger on the board and explains it. You can take the move back and think again.

  • Puzzles

    1,366 beginner puzzles from real games: checkmate in one or two, free pieces, forks and pins. Your puzzle rating grows as you improve.

  • Your mistakes become puzzles

    Every big mistake you make is saved in My Blunder Bank, so you can practise the moment until you find the better move.

  • A reason to come back

    A daily goal, a puzzle of the day, a day streak, levels and badges keep your practice going.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There are no ads, no accounts and nothing to buy.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your games, puzzles and progress are saved in your own browser. You can move them to another device with a backup file in Settings.

Does it work on my phone, and offline?

Yes. On a phone you can add Second Look to your home screen like an app. After your first visit, the bots, lessons and puzzles also work without internet.

I have never played chess. Where do I start?

Open Learn and start with the first lesson, The rook. Each lesson takes a minute or two. When you know the pieces, play the Rookie bot with Coach Guard switched on.
